# specs/

Specs for work not yet built, and shipped ones kept until the issue that cites
them closes. A spec rides with its first pull request and is otherwise deleted
once the screens ship; settled decisions move to
[`docs/decisions.md`](../docs/decisions.md). A `<spec>/design/` folder travels
with its parent. Direction is in [`docs/roadmap.md`](../docs/roadmap.md).

The specs that remain:

- [`android-shell.md`](android-shell.md): the Android app on the shared core
- [`background-audio-plugin.md`](background-audio-plugin.md): the click in the background, prior art for #1600
- [`chart-to-scaffold.md`](chart-to-scaffold.md): exercises from a chord chart
- [`exercise-variations.md`](exercise-variations.md): variations, sections and plays
- [`getting-cold-signal.md`](getting-cold-signal.md): "not practised lately"
- [`icloud-sync.md`](icloud-sync.md): iPhone and iPad sync
- [`live-activity-plugin.md`](live-activity-plugin.md): lock screen progress, prior art for #1600
- [`one-pass-create.md`](one-pass-create.md): a piece with its chart and exercises in one save
- [`one-tap-start.md`](one-tap-start.md): today's plan in one tap
- [`piece-from-photo.md`](piece-from-photo.md): a piece from a photo of the page
- [`planned-item.md`](planned-item.md): one planned-item shape for sessions, routines and the week
- [`practice-defaults.md`](practice-defaults.md): default rep target and click start
- [`practice-instruments.md`](practice-instruments.md): timer, rep counter and click
- [`priority-items.md`](priority-items.md): priority items in place of goals
- [`session-delete.md`](session-delete.md): deleting a saved session
- [`up-next-card.md`](up-next-card.md): the Practice tab suggestion
