An exercise stands on its own, and says which pieces it serves

Today the exercise screen carries a ↵ Related to <one piece> line under the score ring. The arrow reads as “child of”, a second and third piece collapse into · +2 more behind a hidden menu, and an exercise with no piece at all gets no line, so “not linked” is indistinguishable from “nothing to say”. Meanwhile a separate By piece card lower down lists where the exercise has actually been practised. Two lists, two truths, neither complete.

This mock replaces both with one Used in card: every piece the exercise serves, whether it came from a link you made or from practice you did, always present, editable from here.

The same sheet, from the other side. The piece screen already opens a multi-select picker to choose exercises. This is that component with pieces in it, opened from the exercise. Ticking sends the LinkExercise event that already exists, so no new core event is needed.

What changes

  • Used in replaces both the breadcrumb and By piece. One row per piece, merged from links and practice history, always shown.
  • Three row states: practised (ring), linked, not practised yet (rest glyph), practised but not linked (ring plus a Link button).
  • Empty state is a real state: “On its own”, with the link action attached to it.
  • Link a piece from the exercise side, reusing the piece screen’s picker sheet.
  • Reused as-is: ScoreRing (120 and 44, including its unrated rest), SectionHeader, HairlineDivider, CardSurface, the multi-select picker sheet. No new primitives, no new tokens.
  • Untouched: the focus player’s ↵ breadcrumb during play. There the piece is the block’s anchor and genuinely singular, so it stays.
  • Cut: the hidden ⌄ menu behind “+2 more”. Nothing that matters lives behind a disclosure this small.
