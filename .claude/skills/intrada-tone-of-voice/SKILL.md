## Tone of voice

The writing rules for every user-facing string, and the dated Decisions log
(V1 onward), live in `docs/tone-of-voice.md`. Read it before writing or
reviewing a title, button, label, empty state, error, alert, or accessibility
label; its sweep checklist is the review pass for any PR that adds or changes
a string. Where a rule fights a real screen, that is a decision, not a
licence: settle it and append to the log there.

This is the writing layer of `skill://intrada-design-principles`: that skill's
doc decides what a screen does, this one decides what it says. Where they
disagree, the design principle wins and the tone doc gets a new example.
