Paper and ink

What the iOS app draws today, taken from Theme.swift. Warm paper, near-neutral ink, one highlighter the musician picks, and white cards with 3pt corners and a faint lift. This page is a picture of the app, not a proposal.

The eight highlighters

The musician picks one; it is the only bright colour in the app. Butter is the default. It fills primary buttons, the hero's start button, the swipe under page titles and the celebration glyph, always with ink on it. A highlighter is never text or an outline.

The Practice hero card runs from ink at the top trailing corner to the highlighter's own deep stop at the bottom leading corner. The ratio on each card is ink on the highlighter fill.

Typography

Hanken Grotesk for titles and body, DM Mono for figures. Five sizes, 30, 20, 17, 15 and 13; weight and colour carry the hierarchy inside a size. Every style scales with Dynamic Type from its anchor.

Nothing is set in capitals. Every label, a section's title, a field's label or a toggle's title, is label in secondary ink, in sentence case, at the top of what it names; SectionTitle and FieldLabel look the same, and a field's label sits inside its card above the value (FieldCard). Values, and anything read or set, are 17 in ink. Only the dark Practice and Up next cards keep their labels light on dark. Mono is for a figure set alone, a clock, a duration or a count; a line that mixes words and numbers is secondary. The display numerals timer and scoreNumeral are the only styles sized by the dial or readout they sit in. The chart specimen uses the browser's monospace; the app uses the system face.
